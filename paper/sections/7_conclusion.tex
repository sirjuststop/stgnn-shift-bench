\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This audit does not identify a universally robust traffic STGNN. It identifies
a failure coordinate. Sensor-dropout fragility tracks adaptive-adjacency
concentration (Pearson $\rho{=}{+}0.974$ across six adaptive architectures on
METR-LA, with positive associations on three source datasets);
mask-aware inference recovers
$75\%$ of its gap without retraining. Cross-city and temporal evaluation
reveal different effects: MegaCRN leads source-normalized transfer, while
MegaCRN's and STAEformer's weekday/weekend gaps reverse sign under masked-mph
reporting. Model assessment should state both the expected shift and the
metric convention; the frozen-checkpoint protocol makes these distinctions
visible.
