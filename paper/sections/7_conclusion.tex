\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Sensor-dropout sensitivity tracks adaptive-adjacency
concentration (Pearson $\rho{=}{+}0.974$ across six adaptive architectures on
METR-LA, with positive associations on three source datasets);
mask-aware inference recovers
$75\%$ of GWNet's gap without retraining. Cross-city and temporal evaluation
give different rankings: MegaCRN leads source-normalized transfer, while
MegaCRN's and STAEformer's weekday/weekend gaps reverse sign under masked-mph
reporting. Model assessment should specify both the expected shift and the
metric used.
