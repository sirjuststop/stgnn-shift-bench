\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across six adaptive architectures trained on METR-LA, the retained aggregate
results associate sensitivity to silent zero-filling with routing concentration
(Pearson $\rho{=}{+}0.974$); the association is positive but weaker on PEMS-BAY
and PEMS04. For Graph WaveNet, mask-aware inference recovers $75\%$ of the
dropout gap without retraining when failed sensors are known. Cross-city and
temporal evaluation give different rankings: MegaCRN leads source-normalized
transfer, and the sign of MegaCRN's and STAEformer's weekday/weekend gap
depends on whether errors use normalized units or masked mph. Robustness
claims for traffic STGNNs should name both the shift and the metric.
