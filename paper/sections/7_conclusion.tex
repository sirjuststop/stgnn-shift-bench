\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This audit does not identify a universally robust traffic STGNN. It identifies
a failure coordinate. Sensor-dropout fragility tracks adaptive-adjacency
concentration (Pearson $\rho{=}{+}0.979$ on METR-LA, $+0.910$ on PEMS04);
GWNet is the high-concentration endpoint, and mask-aware inference recovers
$75\%$ of its gap without retraining. Cross-city and temporal fragility sit on
separate axes: MegaCRN leads zero-shot transfer yet carries $8$--$9{\times}$
DCRNN's temporal subgroup gap. Model choice should follow the target shift type
and routing concentration; the submission includes the frozen-checkpoint
protocol and recommendation artifact.
