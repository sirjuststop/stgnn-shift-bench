\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

No architecture dominates all three deployment axes. Sensor-dropout fragility
scales monotonically with adaptive-adjacency concentration
(Pearson $\rho{=}{+}0.979$ on METR-LA, $+0.910$ on PEMS04, four adaptive
backbones each); GWNet's $4.3{\times}$ gap over DCRNN is the visible tail of
that relation, and mask-aware inference recovers $75\%$ of it without
retraining. Cross-city and temporal fragility sit on orthogonal axes: MegaCRN
leads zero-shot transfer yet carries $8$--$9{\times}$ larger temporal subgroup
gaps, a failure mode that is not driven by explicit temporal covariates and
that the five-model evaluation could not have surfaced. Model choice should
follow target shift type and routing density of the candidate; multi-shift
assessment should be standard practice. The frozen-checkpoint protocol
reproduces end-to-end on a single commodity GPU and is released with a
recommendation artifact.
